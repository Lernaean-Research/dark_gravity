## Strategic Principles Applied

1. **No Citation Bombing:** Maximum 2-3 citations per sentence to maintain readability
2. **Thematic Clustering:** Grouping related citations at natural conceptual boundaries
3. **Hierarchical Depth:** Foundational references (Einstein, Wald) in theory sections; empirical references (SPARC, surveys) in data/results sections
4. **Cross-Section Reinforcement:** Key foundational references (Carroll, Wald, PoissonWill) cited multiple times at conceptually distinct locations
5. **Observational Trajectory:** Direct detection experiments early (motivation); future surveys late (validation roadmap)
6. **Method Transparency:** Mathematical methods cited at exact point of application (boundary layers, spectral analysis)
